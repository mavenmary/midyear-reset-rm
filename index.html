The Richer Retailer Method

Five Systems.
One Honest Look.

Most store owners white-knuckle through problems instead of stopping to look at what is actually happening. Today we stop and look across all five core systems of your business.

Each petal of the Richer Retailer Method represents a system of your store. When all five are healthy and aligned, you become a truly Richer Retailer.

01
RETAILMavensCash Flow Engine™
Show me the money.
Are your sales translating to cash? Pull your Q1 + Q2 numbers, compare to last year, calculate your IMU, and identify your number one cash flow lever for Q3 + Q4. Target IMU: 60% — even a few points can mean thousands more in profit.
02
RETAILMavensInventory Engine™
Is your inventory working for you?
Dead stock is cash trapped on shelves or in your back room. Assess whether you are over or under-inventoried, then make a decision on every slow mover: Cancel, Reorder, Clear, or Hold.
03
RETAILMavensTeam Engine™
Are you leading your team or reacting to it?
You cannot scale alone. Count your team meetings and 1:1 reviews from Q1 + Q2 and compare to last year. If the number is lower, you are less connected to your team than 12 months ago — and the fix starts this week.
04
RETAILMavensMarketing Engine™
Awareness doesn't pay bills. Offers do.
Count your emails and social posts from Q1 + Q2 and check every one for a clear CTA. The minimum standard: two emails a week, four social posts a week, with a direct ask in every single one.
05
RETAILMavensMindset Engine™
Data over drama.
Are you operating from strategy or scarcity? The CEO operating system: CEO Thinking, Decision Velocity, and data over drama. Plus WEIP — the protected hours every week where you work on the business, not in it. Minimum goal: two hours a week.
